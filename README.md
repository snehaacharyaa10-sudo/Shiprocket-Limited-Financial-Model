# Shiprocket-Limited-Financial-Model
Overview

This project presents an integrated 3-statement financial model for Shiprocket, covering historical financials and projections through FY31.

The model focuses on understanding Shiprocket's evolving business mix, particularly the distinction between its Core and Emerging revenue streams, and translates operating assumptions into revenue, profitability, working capital and cash-flow forecasts.

Key Highlights
Historical + forecast period: FY23–FY31
Revenue segmentation: Core Business and Emerging Business
Financial statements: Income Statement, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow Statement
Supporting schedules: Revenue build-up, working capital, depreciation, fixed assets and other operating assumptions
Profitability analysis: EBITDA, EBIT and PAT
Forecasting: Revenue growth, margins, working-capital days and tax assumptions
Model structure: Assumptions → Revenue Build → P&L → Balance Sheet → Cash Flow
Objective

The objective of the model is to evaluate how Shiprocket's business mix, revenue growth and operating leverage could translate into future profitability and cash generation.

Rather than forecasting revenue as a single line item, the model uses a segment-driven approach to reflect the different growth characteristics of Core and Emerging businesses.

Sources
